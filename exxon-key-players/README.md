# ExxonMobil key players: pilot pass (Oct 21, 2026)

First test of the LinkedIn / Sales Navigator comb for the Cognition Industrial & Energy territory plan. Read-only: no connection requests, messages, saves or InMails. Source for every line below is the person's own LinkedIn profile, self-reported, and only ExxonMobil roles that were active on or after Oct 2023 are used. Past-employer experience is left out.

## How the list was built

1. Sales Navigator > ExxonMobil account > "View all employees" (74K+ leads).
2. Filters: Current company = ExxonMobil, Seniority = Director + Vice President + CXO.
3. First try added a broad Boolean keyword string (software engineering, architecture, cloud, modernization, transformation, legacy, cybersecurity, DevOps, Splunk, Datadog, Dynatrace). That returned 162 leads but was noisy: marketing, trading and market-development titles matched on "transformation" and "architecture". Dropped it.
4. Final filter: Function = Information Technology on top of the seniority filter. 29 leads. 27 profiles were opened; 1 was not visible, and 2 page-2 results were left unopened as low confidence.
5. Per profile: current role, About, ExxonMobil experience entries and descriptions, recent LinkedIn activity (90 days).

## What to know first

- No profile mentions Splunk, Datadog or Dynatrace. Observability and monitoring tooling is not visible on LinkedIn for this group. It will have to come from job posts, conference talks, vendor case studies or direct discovery.
- Concrete project language exists for only a handful of people. Most senior profiles list titles with no descriptions.
- Two top-level targets are restricted in Sales Navigator and need a manual look: Steve Comstock (CIO, Upstream) and William Cirioli (Vice President Global Technology). Neither profile showed any experience detail.
- 99 leads sit under the Engineering function at the same seniority; not reviewed yet.
